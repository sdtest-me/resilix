## Key Insight
Resilix is evolving from "Spiral Dynamics scanner" → "organizational signal intelligence layer".

## Structural Risk
Hidden mixing of interpretive ontology (Spiral Dynamics) + factual inference.
Enterprise buyers may read: "AI objectively detects human developmental stages" → epistemic explosion.

## Recommended Architecture (4 layers)
1. Observable Signals (facts only: hiring language, ESG wording, turnover)
2. Signal Classification (defensible abstractions: hierarchy vs autonomy emphasis)
3. Organizational Pattern Hypotheses (inference: dependency concentration, fragmentation)
4. Optional Interpretive Framework (vMEME overlays — advanced/analyst mode only)

## Strongest Idea
**Contradiction detection**: ESG says "collaboration" (GREEN) + Hiring says "aggressive KPIs" (ORANGE) = organizational incoherence.
→ Defensible, measurable, traceable, executive-ready.

## Dangerous Claim
"72% → 94% accuracy" looks like manufactured precision.
→ Reframe as: "Sponsor Lab improves organizational signal resolution by calibrating public inference against internally validated indicators."

## Category Positioning
Avoid category confusion (HR analytics / culture consulting / ESG).
→ Position as: **organizational resilience intelligence** (board-level urgency).
