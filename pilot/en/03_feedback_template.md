# Resilix Pilot Feedback Template
Version: 0.1 (Pilot)
Audience: HR Directors, COOs, Organizational Development Consultants
---
# 1. Purpose of This Feedback Template
This template is designed to collect structured pilot feedback about:
- interface clarity
- evidence provenance trust
- epistemic transparency
- perceived organizational usefulness
- calibration interest
- objection patterns
- confusion points
The purpose of this pilot is NOT to validate predictive capability.
The purpose is to evaluate whether the Resilix approach:
- communicates observable organizational patterns clearly
- maintains epistemic honesty
- supports operational discussion
- creates trust through traceable inference
- provides useful organizational review signals
This template includes:
1. Structured rating questions
2. Open-ended qualitative questions
3. Trust perception checks
4. Objection identification prompts
5. Calibration interest indicators

# 9. Internal Pilot Review Notes (For Resilix Team)
During pilot review analysis, prioritize:
- repeated confusion points
- predictive expectation leakage
- misunderstanding of interpretive overlay
- trust reactions to evidence provenance
- calibration readiness signals
- governance/privacy objections
Critical warning signs include:
- repeated requests for prediction certainty
- expectation of employee-level analysis
- confusion between interpretive overlay and scientific measurement
- assumptions that the system is adaptive or self-learning
Pilot success depends more on:
- trust clarity
- governance confidence
- operational usefulness
- evidence transparency
than interface aesthetics or perceived sophistication.
