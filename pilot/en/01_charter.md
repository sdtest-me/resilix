# Resilix Pilot Charter
Version: 0.1 (Pilot)
Status: Draft for External Pilot Review
Audience: HR Directors, COOs, Organizational Development Consultants
Product: Resilix — Organizational Resilience Evidence Review System
---
# 1. Pilot Purpose
This pilot evaluates whether Resilix provides a useful, trustworthy, and operationally understandable organizational resilience review process for enterprise stakeholders.
The pilot is designed to test:
- Clarity of the interface and terminology
- Trust in evidence provenance and traceable inference
- Usefulness of observable organizational pattern detection
- Understanding of epistemic limitations
- Interest in internal signal calibration workflows
- Organizational willingness to operationalize stabilization steps
This pilot is NOT intended to validate predictive capability, probabilistic forecasting, or psychometric assessment outcomes.
Resilix is a deterministic reasoning system focused on organizational traceability and observable operational patterns.
---
# 2. What Resilix Is
Resilix is a rules-based organizational resilience review system built around:
- Public ESG disclosures
- Job-market signals
- Governance communications
- Observable dependency concentration patterns
- Deterministic reasoning
- Traceable inference
- Evidence provenance
The system generates structured organizational observations using fixed rules, documented derivation paths, and provenance-linked evidence structures.
Every derived output references:
- `triggered_by[]`
- `derived_from[]`
- evidence provenance chains
- inference depth limits
The system is designed to constrain unsupported interpretation while improving organizational traceability.
---
# 3. What Resilix Is NOT
Resilix is NOT:
- An AI prediction system
- A forecasting engine
- A psychometric assessment platform
- A behavioral profiling system
- An employee surveillance tool
- A diagnostic authority
- A probabilistic risk engine
- A black-box scoring model
Resilix does NOT:
- infer personality traits
- classify individuals
- generate hidden scores
- produce adaptive machine-learning outputs
- guarantee outcomes
- establish objective truth claims
All outputs should be interpreted as structured evidence reviews of observable organizational patterns.
---
# 4. Intended Use Cases
This pilot is intended for the following enterprise use cases:
## Organizational Resilience Review
Identifying observable operational fragility indicators using publicly available organizational signals.
## Dependency Mapping
Reviewing observable dependency concentration patterns that may create operational vulnerability under organizational stress conditions.
## Coordination Gap Review
Surfacing observable inconsistencies between governance communication, hiring patterns, operational structure, and strategic messaging.
## Executive Discussion Support
Providing structured inputs for leadership discussions regarding organizational resilience and stabilization priorities.
## Calibration Preparation
Determining whether internal evidence alignment sessions may improve evidence coverage and organizational interpretability.
---
# 5. Prohibited Use Cases
Resilix must NOT be used for:
## Individual Employee Evaluation
No employee-level analysis, ranking, classification, or performance evaluation is permitted.
## Hiring Decisions
Outputs must not influence recruitment filtering, promotion decisions, compensation review, or disciplinary processes.
## Psychological Interpretation
Spiral Dynamics and related overlays are interpretive overlays only and must not be treated as diagnostic conclusions.
## Strategic Forecasting
Outputs must not be interpreted as forecasts of future organizational events or operational outcomes.
## Compliance Enforcement
Resilix outputs are not legal, regulatory, financial, or audit determinations.
## Covert Organizational Surveillance
The system must not be used for hidden monitoring or unauthorized employee observation.
---
# 6. Epistemic Limitations
Resilix intentionally constrains interpretation depth.
The system prioritizes:
- traceability over sophistication
- structure over semantic speculation
- deterministic reasoning over adaptive inference
- provenance over narrative authority
## Evidence Coverage
During this pilot, most reviews use:
`Evidence coverage: partial (public signals only)`
This means outputs are derived from externally observable organizational evidence and may not reflect:
- internal process documentation
- operational context unavailable publicly
- internal governance workflows
- informal coordination structures
Internal calibration sessions may improve alignment between external signals and internal organizational evidence.
## Inference Depth
Resilix currently enforces:
`Inference depth: 2`
This limits speculative chaining and reduces unsupported interpretation expansion.
The system intentionally avoids deep inferential cascades.
## Interpretive Overlay Constraints
Spiral Dynamics visualizations within Resilix are:
- interpretive overlays
- non-diagnostic conceptual models
- organizational framing tools
They are NOT scientific measurements or psychometric classifications.
Percentages shown in overlays represent relative interpretive emphasis within observable organizational communication patterns.

# 7. Data Privacy & Governance Commitments
## Aggregate-Only Analysis
Resilix enforces aggregate-only organizational review.
The system prohibits:
- individual profiling
- employee-level inference
- personal behavioral classification
- identity-linked organizational scoring
Minimum aggregation thresholds are enforced before internal organizational evidence may be processed.
## Namespace Isolation
Calibration sessions use isolated organizational namespaces.
Evidence submitted during calibration:
- remains organization-specific
- is not merged across clients
- is not reused for external inference generation
## Confidential Calibration
Internal calibration evidence is treated as confidential operational material.
Calibration sessions are intended to improve:
- evidence alignment
- organizational context interpretation
- operational traceability
Calibration is not intended to create hidden predictive models.
